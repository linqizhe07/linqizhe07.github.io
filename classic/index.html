PublicationSole authorOct 2025 — May 2026

FORGE: Fine-Tuning Over Reward Generations through Evolution

Rewards and policies evolve together: an LLM writes the reward functions, and pretrained successor features let policies adapt to each new reward instantly — no retraining. Conceived and built end-to-end, solo.

New state of the art among fully automated LLM reward-search methods on three MuJoCo benchmarks — beating the prior best at one-tenth the compute.

Learned Dispatch Policies for Multi-Line Task Scheduling

Taught three different policies — supervised, LLM, and RL — to schedule a multi-robot assembly line, then proved with queueing theory why none of them can beat First-Come-First-Served, and exactly what would change that.
